date,time,duration,distance,avg_heart_rate,avg_pace,elevation,avg_power,avg_cadence,temperature

8. avg_power   -> integer W, e.g. 151
9. avg_cadence -> integer SPM, e.g. 161
10. temperature-> integer Celsius, e.g. 25 (from the TEMP value "25 degrees";
